ABOUT THE CLUB

We are UTAR's home for Counter-Strike 2 — a place to watch pro matches together, chat about the game, and ease your way into friendly 5v5s with people who get it.

// Who We Are

MISSION & VALUES

Watch Together

Big screen, snacks, and a room full of people yelling at the same clutch. We run watch parties for every Major and top-tier tournament — no need to know the meta, just come hang out.

Build a Lasting Community

Beyond the game, we're a social hub for CS2 fans at UTAR. Weekly chill sessions, watch parties, and a 200+ member Discord for chit-chatting, memes, and match talk year-round.

A Path to Friendly Matches

Once you're comfortable in the community, there's a low-pressure way into 5v5 scrims and casual tournaments — no tryouts, no pressure, just teammates figuring it out together.

// The Origin

HOW IT ALL STARTED

SEPTEMBER 2023

01 THE BEGINNING

It began the way most things in university do — in a group chat at 2 AM.

Five students from different faculties had one thing in common: they all played CS2 competitively but had nowhere to compete as a team. The university had football clubs, debate teams, and music societies. But for competitive gaming? Nothing. So they made one.

WEEK ONE

02 THE FIRST MEETING

They booked Room B304, capacity 30. Expected 10 people. 47 students walked in. Some brought their own keyboards. Two professors came out of curiosity. That night, they didn't just talk about gaming — they talked about what was missing: no organized competition, no training environment, no community.

The club was officially registered the following week.

2023 — 2024

03 YEAR ONE — BUILDING THE FOUNDATION

The first year was chaotic and beautiful.

Weekly
Friday Night Scrims
5v5 internal matches. 20+ players every week without fail.
Month 3
First Tournament
CS2 invitational between three universities. Nexus placed second. The loss lit a fire.
Month 6
Coaching & Content
Ex-semi-pro joined for demo reviews. Highlight reels launched on Instagram.

By year's end, Nexus had 72 registered members.

2024 — 2025

04 YEAR TWO — GROWING TOGETHER

Watch parties got bigger, and so did the Discord. Some regulars wanted more than chit-chat, so the club launched a casual internal league — friendly 5v5s, no tryouts, no pressure — plus the campus's first LAN watch event with 120 attendees.

It was still a hangout at heart. Just a bigger, louder one.

05 WHAT WE DO TODAY

Watch Parties

Majors, weekly HLTV matches, and big-screen viewings on campus — pop in, no commitment needed.

Community

A 200+ member Discord for chit-chat, memes, match talk, and welcoming new faces year-round.

Friendly Matches

Casual internal 5v5s and scrims — a relaxed on-ramp for anyone curious about playing together.

Events & Ops

LAN watch nights, internal leagues, and campus-wide gaming events open to everyone.

Content & Media

Highlight clips, casual recaps, social media, and live-stream commentary from the group.
